Reflection: Jake successfully navigated his first energy switch. The hardest part wasn't the provider process — which was largely smooth and digital — but the coordination overhead of getting housemates aligned and establishing fair payment routines. The experience would have been significantly improved by built-in bill-splitting and clearer guidance for shared households.
