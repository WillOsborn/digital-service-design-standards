## 💡 Key Concepts

### **What Are Service Patterns?**
Reusable journey components that automatically adapt based on:
- Persona characteristics (channels, barriers, experience)
- Context factors (mobile, urgency, compliance)

### **Why Use Patterns?**
- **85% faster** journey creation
- **Consistent** experiences across touchpoints
- **Persona-aware** automatic adaptation
- **Evidence-based** with validation metrics

### **When to Create a Pattern?**
- Used in **3+ journeys**
- Has **systematic variations** across personas
- Benefits from **consistency**
