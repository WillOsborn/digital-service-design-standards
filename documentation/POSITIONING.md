## 🎯 Core Positioning

### What We Are
**Digital Service Design Schemas establish the professional standard for evidence-based service design practice.**

### What We're Not
- NOT an "enhancement" to existing standards (no prior industry standard existed)
- NOT a "better version" of something else
- NOT just another persona template

### Key Message
**We're establishing THE standard, not improving upon one.**

> "Service designers create personas in Word documents, PowerPoint slides, spreadsheets—countless inconsistent formats that don't integrate with tools and lose value over time. We've established the first comprehensive professional standard for persona and journey schemas. Our system captures 9 core behavioral attributes including systematic barrier analysis (why problems exist) and multi-channel orchestration. The structured JSON format enables tool integration, automated validation, and cross-team collaboration. Teams adopting our schemas move from ad-hoc persona creation to systematic, evidence-based design practice with measurable outcomes. This isn't enhancing existing standards—we're creating the standard the industry needed."

## 💡 When Asked About Alternatives

### If someone asks: "How is this different from [other tool/template]?"

**Response Framework:**
1. Acknowledge the tool/template exists
2. Explain we're establishing an industry standard (format/structure) not a tool
3. Emphasize interoperability and ecosystem potential
4. Note our comprehensive behavioral focus

**Example:**
> "We're not competing with [tool X] - we're establishing the underlying standard format that tools like [tool X] could adopt for interoperability. Our focus is creating comprehensive, structured schemas that capture behavioral depth and enable ecosystem development. Think of us as establishing the 'file format standard' while tools provide the 'authoring experience.'"
